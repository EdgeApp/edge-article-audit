To view your transaction:

1.  Tap "Assets" located within the bottom navigation bar
    
2.  Tap on the wallet that you want the transaction ID.
    
3.  Tap the transaction that you want the ID for
    
4.  Inside the transaction, you will see the transaction ID. Tap the ID to copy.
    
5.  You can also scroll down and tap "Show in Explorer"
    

You can copy and paste the Transaction ID by tapping the copy and paste button into any explorer.
