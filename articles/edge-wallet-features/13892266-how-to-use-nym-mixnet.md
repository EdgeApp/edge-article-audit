The Nym Mixnet feature allows you to privatize your transactions by routing traffic through a decentralized network, effectively masking your IP address and data.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Open Edge** and tap the **Menu** icon (three lines) in the upper right corner.
    
2.  Scroll down to the **Options** section and select **Privacy Settings**.
    
3.  **Toggle on** the switch next to the specific asset you wish to privatize (e.g., Monero).
    
4.  **Go to the Asset:** Tap on the asset you just toggled on to begin sending or receiving funds privately.
    

**Important Note:** Because the Nym Mixnet can significantly slow down network traffic and sync speeds, we recommend enabling it for **one asset at a time**. We are actively working on improvements to both Edge and the Nym Mixnet to increase speed and performance in future updates.
